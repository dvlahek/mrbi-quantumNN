# Full-campaign multiscale stage audit — 28 September 2026

## Scope and provenance

Exploratory, label-free diagnosis of the completed **full** 97-method,
45-job corrected-continuation campaign. The separate stage-audit checkout
used the full campaign's original package environment and matched its
fixed `forced_full_balanced_qnn` numerical configuration. For each of
9 tasks and 5 seeds, it used the first 8 **training** inputs after the
original seeded split and PCA transform. No QNN was retrained.

Files generated by the independent checkout:
`outputs/multiscale_stage_audit_v1/stage_raw.csv`,
`paired_sample_results.csv` and `stage_summary.csv`.
They contain 3,600 stage records and 360 paired training-input records,
with no missing values or duplicate sample/stage keys. Recomputed
summaries match the supplied summary to floating-point precision.
The underlying source CSVs and shard manifests remain on the Ryzen
workstation and must be deposited with any reproducibility package.

## Observations

- The final candidate distance between continuation and repeated
  final-sigma optimization is at most 0.001 for **352 of 360** inputs;
  median distance is `7.21e-6`. Eight cases diverge more, including
  large outliers; do not describe the methods as exactly identical.
- Two inputs have candidate-root success for continuation and not
  for the repeated-final-sigma control. In both cases the zero-start
  root solver already succeeds. The **accepted** solver-success
  difference is zero for all 360 inputs. No pair has two distinct
  *converged* roots by the audit's criterion.
- Continuation averages 2,754 optimizer objective evaluations per
  input versus 2,189 for repeated final sigma: approximately **25.8%**
  extra work. Extra stage-audit diagnostic root solves are not included
  in those objective counts and instrumented wall time should not be
  compared to the original uninstrumented ablation.
- Continuation's first stage moves the candidate by mean norm 2.274;
  stage 2 moves it by 0.0104 and stage 5 by `8.6e-7`. The
  continuation stage-root success count is 188/360 at stages 1 and
  5. Two individual stage-root statuses flip after stage 1, one in
  each direction; no changes occur after stage 2. Repeated final
  sigma records 186/360 at all stages with no status flips.
- The mean weighted detector term in continuation decreases from
  0.00696 at sigma 0.70 to 0.00127 at the final sigma 0.02. At the
  final scale the mean residual term is 0.14560. The ratio of these
  **means** is 0.87%; this does not bound the detector contribution
  to individual inputs or prove it has no effect.

The earlier 45-pair QNN final-sigma ablation yielded a per-task mean
balanced-accuracy difference of +0.0023 for continuation minus
repeated final sigma, exploratory two-sided p=0.5703, with more
objective calls for continuation. The 360 training inputs here are
*diagnostic samples*, not 360 independent datasets or a new QNN
significance test.

## Decision and next development experiment

The existing continuation schedule typically converges to nearly the
same final-scale candidate as the control, after more optimization.
Changing the number of scales or increasing iteration limits without
changing the objective does not address that observed mechanism.

A **single opt-in scale-dependent smoothed-residual homotopy** is the
next development candidate. In early stages replace a specified
fraction of the leading `||F(z)||` contribution with
`||mean_U F(z + sigma U)||`, using a predeclared schedule; remove
the smoothed fraction at the final scale. Keep the actual zero-set
equation, root solver, QNN architecture, splits and training unchanged.
Do not simultaneously retune other MRBI weights or select a profile
using test accuracy. Reuse common antithetic probes in both arms to
reduce Monte Carlo confounding, and evaluate probe coupling alone
as a separate control if its effect matters.

Measure true objective evaluation and root-solve budgets, candidate
distance, stage-root success, accepted success and residual before
running any new QNN. Compare with a repeated-final-sigma arm with
the **same** stage schedule, coefficients, probe estimator and actual
allowed evaluation budget. Predefine outcomes and reserve new seeds
or external tasks for confirmation. If budget-matched root success
does not improve, retain the measured negative result and frame
solver-aware initialization, not multiscale superiority, as the
paper's contribution.
